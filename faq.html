Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our event planning and management services.

What Services Does Regan Feste Provide?

Regan Feste Events & Weddings offers complete event management services including Wedding Planning, Corporate Events, Birthday Celebrations, Engagement Ceremonies, Private Parties, Theme Decorations, Mantaps & Backdrops, Entertainment, Hospitality, and Customized Event Planning.

How Far In Advance Should I Book My Event?

We recommend booking your event at least 1 to 3 months in advance. For weddings and large-scale celebrations, booking 3 to 6 months ahead ensures better planning and vendor availability.

Do You Provide Customized Event Packages?

Yes. Every event is unique, and we create customized packages based on your requirements, guest count, venue, theme preferences, and budget.

Do You Handle Venue Decoration And Setup?

Absolutely. Our team manages complete venue decoration including floral arrangements, stage setups, lighting, entrance décor, seating arrangements, and customized theme decorations.

Can You Arrange Entertainment For Events?

Yes. We provide DJs, live bands, anchors, performers, dancers, games, and other entertainment options based on the event type and client preferences.

Do You Offer Services Outside Bengaluru?

Yes. Regan Feste can manage events across Karnataka and other cities depending on the event requirements and location.

How Can I Get A Quotation?

You can request a quotation through our Contact Form, WhatsApp, Phone Call, or Email. Once we understand your event requirements, we will provide a customized quotation.

What Information Do You Need To Plan An Event?

To get started, we typically require Event Type, Event Date, Venue Location, Estimated Guest Count, Budget Range, Theme Preferences, and any special requirements.

Do You Provide Catering Services?

Yes. We work with trusted catering partners and can arrange vegetarian, non-vegetarian, traditional, and customized menu options for events of all sizes.

Why Choose Regan Feste Events & Weddings?

Our team combines creativity, professionalism, and attention to detail to deliver memorable celebrations. From planning to execution, we ensure a seamless and stress-free experience for every client.
