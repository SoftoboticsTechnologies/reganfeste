Who Are We

Established in the year of 2024, Regan Feste is a master at turning dreams into reality, crafting unforgettable celebrations with an artful blend of creativity, elegance, and flawless execution.

Specializing in a wide range of occasions—be it weddings, private parties, or corporate events—Regan ensures each event is a beautifully curated experience that resonates with style, emotion, and unforgettable memories.

Our Mission

To deliver an unforgettable experience to our customers by providing a worry-free event through outstanding quality services and innovative ideas.

Our 6-D Process

  1. 01.

    01. Consultation

    We begin by understanding your vision, requirements, budget, and expectations to create a personalized event plan tailored to your needs.

  2. 02.

    02. Planning

    Our team develops a detailed event strategy, including venue selection, theme concepts, décor planning, vendor coordination, and timelines.

  3. 03.

    03. Design & Décor

    We transform ideas into reality with creative themes, elegant decorations, floral arrangements, stage setups, and customized event experiences.

  4. 04.

    04. Coordination

    We manage vendors, logistics, entertainment, catering, guest arrangements, and every operational detail to ensure smooth execution.

  5. 05.

    05. Execution

    On the event day, our experienced team oversees every aspect, ensuring everything runs seamlessly and according to plan.

  6. 06.

    06. Celebration

    Relax and enjoy your special occasion while we handle the details, creating unforgettable memories for you and your guests.
